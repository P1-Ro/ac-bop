# acbop 1.0.3

The safety car is now a queue behind the leader. The server side is the only thing that changed: the HUD (`acbop_hud_v1.0.0.zip` below) is the same as in 1.0.0, so drivers who already have it don't need to reinstall.

## How the safety car works now

In limiter mode (the default):

- **The leader is capped at 60 km/h** as soon as the safety car is called.
- **Everyone else drives normally until they catch up.** Once a car is within 0.5 s of the car physically in front of it, and that car is already in the queue, it is capped at 60 km/h too. The queue builds from the front, one car at a time, and nobody can pull away from it.
- **It ends when the whole field is in the queue**, or at the time limit.
- A queued car that drops more than 3 s behind the car in front (a spin, a trip off) is let go, so it can catch up again.
- The in-game banner tells each driver where they stand: `YOU LEAD - LIMIT 60 KM/H`, `LIMIT 60 KM/H - stay in line`, or `CATCH UP TO THE QUEUE - 2/4 in line`.
- The in-game cap follows a driver who brakes for the queue themselves, so they can't speed back up into the car in front.
- The Live tab's banner shows how many cars are in line, and each car is shown as **LEADS**, **LIMITED** or **CATCHING UP**.

Unchanged: the caller runs with no handicap until it ends, only the last-placed car can call it, and a car whose game isn't running the limiter is held with the restrictor instead. Restrictor mode keeps the old catch-up behaviour, since the restrictor can't hold a speed.

## New settings

- **Join the queue within** (`vsc_queue_gap_s`, 0.5 s): how close a car must get to the car in front before it is capped.
- **Let go after dropping back** (`vsc_queue_release_s`, 3 s): how far a queued car can fall back before it is let go.

## ⚠️ Change two settings on an existing server

`config.json` stores every setting, so a server set up before this release keeps its old values. In the **Settings** tab, set:

- **Speed limit** to **60** (was 100)
- **Hard time limit** to **300** (was 180). A field spread round most of the lap takes two to three minutes to queue up at 60 km/h.

## Upgrading

Pull `master` (or download this release), restart acbop, and change the two settings above.
